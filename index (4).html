Frequently asked questions

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Some tests, like fasting sugar and lipid profile, need 8 to 12 hours of fasting. Water is fine. Ask us when you book.

How soon will I get my report?

Most common tests are ready the same day. Some tests take 2 to 3 days.

Do you collect samples at home?

Yes. Write your address in the booking form and we will confirm the time.

Can I book a test without a doctor’s prescription?

Yes, you can book many tests directly. For treatment, please show your report to a doctor.

How can I pay?

You can pay by cash or UPI when we collect your sample.

Are my reports private?

Yes. Reports are shared only with you on WhatsApp or email.
